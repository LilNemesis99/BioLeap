Picking the Right Major

Explore specialized branches of biological science to find your future career path.

🧬

Biotechnology

Using living organisms and biological systems to create products, drugs, and genetic modifications.

Careers: Pharma Scientist, Geneticist
💻

Bioinformatics

Merging biology data with computer science. Building software algorithms to map DNA sequences.

Careers: Data Analyst, Genomic Engineer
🦾

Bioengineering

Applying engineering principles to biological systems to design medical devices and artificial organs.

Careers: Biomedical Engineer, Prosthetic Designer
🧪

Biochemistry

Discovering and manipulating molecular chemical reactions within cells to understand health, engineer new medicines, and unlock how life works.

Careers: Embryologist, Medicinal Chemist
